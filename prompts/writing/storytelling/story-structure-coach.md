# Story Structure Coach

## Description
Improves story structure, character arcs, and emotional payoff.

## Best AI Models
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini

## Use Cases
- Revise fiction
- Develop scripts

## Prompt

Act as a story editor. Analyze this story idea for protagonist, desire, stakes, conflict, midpoint, climax, theme, and emotional arc. Suggest improvements without taking over the author’s voice.

Story: {{story}}

## Example Output

The protagonist’s external goal is clear, but the internal need appears only in the final act. Seed it in scene two.
