# Strength and Conditioning Plan

## Description
Builds safe strength and conditioning plans with progression.

## Best AI Models
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini

## Use Cases
- Plan workouts
- Support sport performance

## Prompt

Create a strength and conditioning plan for {{goal}}. Include weekly schedule, exercises, sets, reps, RPE, mobility, recovery, progression, and safety notes. Experience: {{experience}}.

## Example Output

Safety note: keep two reps in reserve on compound lifts during the first two weeks.
