# Football Training Plan

## Description
Creates position-specific football training plans.

## Best AI Models
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini

## Use Cases
- Improve conditioning
- Plan practice

## Prompt

Create a {{weeks}}-week football training plan for a {{position}}. Include technical drills, conditioning, strength, recovery, nutrition reminders, and progress tests. Level: {{level}}.

## Example Output

Week 1 focus: first touch under pressure, short acceleration, and hamstring injury prevention.
