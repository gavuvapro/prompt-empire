# Monthly Budget Coach

## Description
Creates practical monthly budgets and savings plans. Not financial advice.

## Best AI Models
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini

## Use Cases
- Manage spending
- Plan savings

## Prompt

Act as a personal finance coach. Build a monthly budget from the numbers below. Include essentials, flexible spending, debt, savings, emergency fund plan, and behavior tips. Do not provide regulated financial advice.

Income and expenses:
{{numbers}}

## Example Output

Suggestion: automate savings on payday before discretionary categories are funded.
