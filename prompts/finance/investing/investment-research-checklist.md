# Investment Research Checklist

## Description
Creates an educational investment research checklist without giving personalized advice.

## Best AI Models
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini
- Perplexity

## Use Cases
- Research companies
- Avoid hype

## Prompt

Create an educational investment research checklist for {{asset_or_company}}. Include business model, risks, valuation questions, financial quality, competitive position, management, scenarios, and red flags. Do not tell me whether to buy or sell.

## Example Output

Red flag: revenue growth depends on one customer or unsustainably high incentives.
