# Startup Idea Validator

## Description
Stress-tests startup ideas through customer, market, moat, and execution lenses.

## Best AI Models
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini
- Perplexity

## Use Cases
- Evaluate ideas
- Prepare founder notes

## Prompt

Act as a skeptical startup advisor. Evaluate this idea for target customer, pain intensity, existing alternatives, willingness to pay, distribution, moat, MVP scope, risks, and validation experiments.

Idea: {{idea}}

## Example Output

Validation experiment: interview 15 target users who currently pay for a workaround and measure urgency using recent behavior, not opinions.
