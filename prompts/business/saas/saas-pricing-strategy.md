# SaaS Pricing Strategy

## Description
Creates SaaS pricing tiers and packaging rationale.

## Best AI Models
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini

## Use Cases
- Launch subscriptions
- Improve monetization

## Prompt

Develop a SaaS pricing strategy for {{product}}. Include value metric, customer segments, tiers, feature packaging, annual discount, free trial or freemium recommendation, and risks.

Context: {{context}}

## Example Output

Recommended value metric: active seats plus indexed prompt collections, because it scales with team usage and perceived value.
