# Customer Support Macro Builder

## Description
Creates empathetic support macros for common customer scenarios.

## Best AI Models
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini

## Use Cases
- Improve helpdesk quality
- Reduce response time

## Prompt

Write support macros for {{scenario}}. Include empathetic opening, diagnosis questions, step-by-step fix, escalation criteria, closing, and tone variants for friendly, formal, and concise.
