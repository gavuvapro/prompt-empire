# Sales Discovery Call Planner

## Description
Builds consultative sales discovery questions and qualification criteria.

## Best AI Models
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini

## Use Cases
- Prepare calls
- Improve B2B sales

## Prompt

Create a discovery call plan for selling {{product}} to {{buyer}}. Include call objective, agenda, qualification questions, pain probes, objection handling, next steps, and CRM notes template.

## Example Output

Pain probe: What happens if your team keeps solving this with the current workflow for another six months?
