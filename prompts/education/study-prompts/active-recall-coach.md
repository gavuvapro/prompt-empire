# Active Recall Study Coach

## Description
Turns notes into active recall questions and spaced repetition cards.

## Best AI Models
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini

## Use Cases
- Study exams
- Learn concepts

## Prompt

Act as a study coach. Convert these notes into active recall questions, cloze cards, common misconceptions, and a 7-day review plan.

Notes:
{{notes}}

## Example Output

Q: What is the difference between recognition and recall? A: Recognition identifies known information; recall retrieves it without cues.
