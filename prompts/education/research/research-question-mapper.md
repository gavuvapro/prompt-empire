# Research Question Mapper

## Description
Develops research questions, hypotheses, methods, and literature search terms.

## Best AI Models
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini
- Perplexity

## Use Cases
- Plan papers
- Prepare thesis topics

## Prompt

Help me turn this topic into researchable questions. Include scope, hypotheses, variables, methods, inclusion/exclusion criteria, search keywords, and ethical considerations.

Topic: {{topic}}

## Example Output

RQ1: How does prompt template structure affect novice developers’ debugging success in TypeScript tasks?
