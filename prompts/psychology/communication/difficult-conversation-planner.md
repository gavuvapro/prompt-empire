# Difficult Conversation Planner

## Description
Prepares respectful difficult conversations with boundaries and scripts.

## Best AI Models
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini

## Use Cases
- Resolve conflict
- Give feedback

## Prompt

Help me prepare for a difficult conversation about {{issue}}. Include goals, facts vs interpretations, empathy statements, boundary language, questions, likely reactions, and a concise script.

## Example Output

Script: “I want to understand what happened and agree on a clearer process for next time.”
