# Udio Music Direction

## Description
Creates Udio prompts for polished music generation.

## Best AI Models
- Udio

## Use Cases
- Create soundtracks
- Explore genres

## Prompt

Create a Udio prompt for {{music_goal}}. Include genre blend, era, tempo, instrumentation, arrangement, vocal direction, production style, and reference-free mood descriptors.

## Example Output

Modern ambient techno, 124 BPM, pulsing bass, glassy arpeggios, spacious mix, optimistic and focused, instrumental.
