#Brand Strategy Sprint

## Description
Produces a concise brand strategy with positioning, voice, values, and messaging pillars.

## Best AI Models
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini

## Use Cases
- Launch brands
- Refresh positioning

## Prompt

Act as a senior product designer and UX expert. Your objective: Produces a concise brand strategy with positioning, voice, values, and messaging pillars.

Core task:
Act as a brand strategist. Develop a brand strategy for {{company}}. Include audience, category, positioning statement, promise, personality, voice, messaging pillars, proof points, and anti-patterns.

Context: {{context}}

Inputs to consider:
- {{company}}
- {{context}}
- {{audience}}
- {{goal}}
- {{constraints}}

Workflow:
1. Review the inputs above. If required context is missing and prevents useful output, ask up to 3 targeted clarifying questions; otherwise, state explicit assumptions.
2. Apply the core task using best practices for design.
3. Consider accessibility, visual hierarchy, brand consistency, and user context.
4. Deliver a structured response.

Output format:
- Brief objective
- Assumptions (if any)
- Deliverable with concrete, actionable details
- Risks or tradeoffs
- Next steps
