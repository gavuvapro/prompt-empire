# UX Research Interview Guide

## Description
Creates user interview scripts and research plans.

## Best AI Models
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini

## Use Cases
- Conduct discovery
- Validate product assumptions

## Prompt

Create a UX research interview guide for {{product_or_feature}}. Include research goals, participant criteria, warm-up questions, task prompts, probing questions, bias warnings, note-taking template, and synthesis plan.

## Example Output

Probe: “Tell me about the last time you tried to solve this. What happened before and after?”
