# Worldbuilding Bible

## Description
Creates a structured worldbuilding bible for fiction, games, or scripts.

## Best AI Models
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini

## Use Cases
- Develop novels
- Plan games

## Prompt

Create a worldbuilding bible for {{setting}}. Include geography, history, cultures, politics, economy, technology or magic, conflicts, daily life, naming conventions, and story hooks.

## Example Output

Story hook: an archivist discovers that the empire’s official prompts rewrite public memory.
